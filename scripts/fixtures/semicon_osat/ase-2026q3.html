ASE Technology Holding Co., Ltd. Reports Its Unaudited Consolidated Financial
Results for the Third Quarter of 2026

 

Taipei, October 29, 2026 – ASE Technology Holding Co., Ltd. (TWSE: 3711, NYSE: ASX) (“We”, “ASEH”, or the “Company”), the leading provider of semiconductor assembly and testing services (“ATM”) and the provider of electronic manufacturing services (“EMS”), today reported its unaudited1 net revenues of NT$244,161 million for 3Q26, up by 44.8% year-over-year and up by 27.8% sequentially. Net income attributable to shareholders of the parent for the quarter totaled NT$26,996 million, up from NT$10,870 million in 3Q25 and up from NT$21,065 million in 2Q26.  Basic earnings per share for the quarter were NT$6.13 (or US$0.384 per ADS), compared to NT$2.50 for 3Q25 and NT$4.80 for 2Q26.  Diluted earnings per share for the quarter were NT$5.84 (or US$0.365 per ADS), compared to NT$2.41 for 3Q25 and NT$4.61 for 2Q26.

 

RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

 

3Q26 Results Highlights – Consolidated

 

lNet revenues from packaging operations, testing operations, EMS operations, and others represented approximately 50%, 11%, 38%, and 1% of the total net revenues for the quarter, respectively.

 

lCost of revenues was NT$190,194 million for the quarter, up from NT$150,917 million in 2Q26.

 

-Raw material cost totaled NT$116,793 million for the quarter, representing 48% of the total net revenues.

 

-Labor cost totaled NT$23,777 million for the quarter, representing 10% of the total net revenues.

 

-Depreciation, amortization and rental expenses totaled NT$20,664 million for the quarter.

 

lGross margin increased by 1.1 percentage points to 22.1% in 3Q26 from 21.0% in 2Q26.

 

lOperating margin was 13.6% in 3Q26, compared to 11.1% in 2Q26.

 

lNon-operating items:

 

-Net interest expense was NT$2,611 million.

 

-Net gain on foreign exchange hedging activities was NT$1,176 million.

3Q26 Results Highlights – ATM

 

lNet revenues were NT$151,485 million for the quarter, up by 51.0% year-over-year and up by 20.1% sequentially.

 

lCost of revenues was NT$105,073 million for the quarter, up by 35.4% year-over-year and up by 14.6% sequentially.

 

-Raw material cost totaled NT$40,974 million for the quarter, representing 27% of the total net revenues.

 

-Labor cost totaled NT$20,013 million for the quarter, representing 13% of the total net revenues.

 

-Depreciation, amortization and rental expenses totaled NT$19,131 million for the quarter.

 

lGross margin increased by 3.3 percentage points to 30.6% in 3Q26 from 27.3% in 2Q26.

 

lOperating margin was 20.0% in 3Q26, compared to 15.7% in 2Q26.

 

3Q26 Results Highlights – EMS

 

lNet revenues were NT$93,469 million, up by 35.4% year-over-year and up by 42.1% sequentially.

 

lCost of revenues for the quarter was NT$85,676 million, up by 36.8% year-over-year and up by 43.0% sequentially.

 

-Raw material cost totaled NT$76,221 million for the quarter, representing 82% of the total net revenues.

 

-Labor cost totaled NT$3,698 million for the quarter, representing 4% of the total net revenues.

 

-Depreciation, amortization and rental expenses totaled NT$1,289 million for the quarter.

 

lGross margin decreased by 0.6 percentage points to 8.3% in 3Q26 from 8.9% in 2Q26.

 

lOperating margin was 3.3% in 3Q26, compared to 2.4% in 2Q26.

Business Review

 

Customers

 

ATM Basis

 

lOur five largest customers together accounted for approximately 46% of our total net revenues in 3Q26, compared to 44% in 2Q26. One customer accounted for more than 10% of our total net revenues in 3Q26.

 

lOur top 10 customers contributed 63% of our total net revenues in 3Q26, compared to 60% in 2Q26.

 

lOur customers that are integrated device manufacturers or IDMs accounted for 46% of our total net revenues in 3Q26, compared to 41% in 2Q26.

 

EMS Basis

 

lOur five largest customers together accounted for approximately 70% of our total net revenues in 3Q26, compared to 58% in 2Q26. Two customers each accounted for more than 10% of our total net revenues in 3Q26 individually.

 

lOur top 10 customers contributed 78% of our total net revenues in 3Q26, compared to 69% in 2Q26.

 

About ASE Technology Holding Co., Ltd. 

ASEH is the leading provider of semiconductor manufacturing services in assembly and test. The Company develops and offers complete turnkey solutions covering front-end engineering test, wafer probing and final test, as well as packaging, materials and electronic manufacturing services through USI with superior technologies, breakthrough innovations, and advanced development programs. With advanced technological capabilities and a global presence spanning Taiwan, China, South Korea, Japan, Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines, Vietnam, Mexico, and Tunisia as well as the United States and Europe, ASEH has established a reputation for reliable, high quality products and services. 

For more information, please visit our website at https://www.aseglobal.com.
